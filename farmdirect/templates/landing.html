How FarmDirect works

1
Farmer lists produce

Crop, grade, quantity, harvest date & price — in under 2 minutes, even on a basic phone. AI suggests the best price.

2
Buyer orders directly

Consumers check out from the marketplace; restaurants & retailers request bulk quotations and compare suppliers.

3
Farmer accepts

Farmers confirm the order, produce is picked up by FarmDirect logistics — payment is secured on pickup.

4
AI delivers smartly

Nearby orders are grouped into optimized routes. Both sides track the delivery live until it reaches the door.
